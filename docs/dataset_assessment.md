## Why the other 28 were excluded

**Never reach 1.4 Ah — no EOL exists (8).**
B0007, B0025, B0027, B0028, B0029, B0030, B0031, B0032.
B0007 is the near-miss: it bottoms out at **1.4005 Ah**, about 0.5 mAh above the
threshold, so it has no EOL under a strict `<= 1.4` rule despite an otherwise textbook
trajectory. B0029–B0032 are 43 °C tests that only fade to 78–83% SoH.

**Cold-temperature derating — never healthy (6).**
B0041, B0045, B0053, B0054, B0055, B0056 run at 4 °C, where capacity never exceeds
1.4 Ah *even when fresh* (max 1.08–1.34 Ah). They start "below EOL" through cold
derating rather than degradation, so the 1.4 Ah threshold is not physically applicable.

**Protocol / regime break (4).**
B0042, B0043, B0044 collapse from ~1.5 Ah to ~0.06 Ah for 46 consecutive cycles
(41–86), then recover to ~1.5 Ah and resume degrading — a test-protocol change, not
degradation. B0033 has a shorter 8-cycle break at 140–147.

**Non-monotonic or too short (10).**
B0038–B0040 (mixed 24/44 °C) have *positive* Spearman ρ — capacity rises over life.
B0026, B0049–B0052 have fewer than 30 valid cycles (B0052 has just 4).

**Spurious EOL after artifact correction (2).**
B0034 and B0036 have a cycle-1 conditioning discharge; B0036 additionally never reaches
a sustained crossing.

## Two artifacts that corrupt naive EOL extraction

These are the traps worth knowing about — a plain "first cycle where `Capacity <= 1.4`"
returns wrong labels on 18 of the 34 batteries.

**1. Cycle-1 conditioning discharge (13 batteries).** The first discharge is partial, not
a real capacity measurement. B0036 reads 1.002 Ah at cycle 1 then 1.801 Ah at cycle 2.
Naive extraction returns `EOL = cycle 1` for all of them; B0034's true sustained crossing
is cycle 76.

**2. Regime breaks (B0042–B0044).** Naive extraction returns `EOL = 41`, the first cycle
of the collapsed block. The real sustained crossings are cycles 103 / 101 / 96.

The assessment script distinguishes the **literal** first crossing from a **confirmed**
sustained crossing (window-median based, tolerant of capacity-regeneration bounce) and
flags the difference in `EOL_reliable`.

## Data-quality flags found

- exact-zero capacity entries: B0042–B0051, B0053, B0054
- empty `Capacity` fields: B0050 (4 cycles), B0052 (21 of 25 cycles)
- capacity above the 2.0 Ah rating: B0006, B0036 (2.44 Ah spike), B0049–B0051 (up to 2.64 Ah)
